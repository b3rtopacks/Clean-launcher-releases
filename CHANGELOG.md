# Changelog

## 0.9.0 beta — first public beta
Internal build `0.9.0-test.4`, versionCode 1004. Tested on Fire TV Stick 4K (2nd Gen, 2023), Fire OS 8.1.8.5.

- Official app icon and Fire TV app-library banner.
- Ad-free app grid with real app icons, D-pad navigation, reorder and hide.
- Cinematic Ambient (default), Minimal Ambient and Classic styles.
- Home button and "Alexa, go home" open Clean Launcher (one-time setup).
- Remote Settings button, Fire TV Settings and setup screens are never redirected.
- Opens automatically after a restart, at most once per boot.
- Known: about 1–1.5 s black transition on Home; "Intercept Home key" option does not work on Fire OS 8;
  after updating, open Clean Launcher once.
